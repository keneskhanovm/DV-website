About the project

As AI rapidly integrates into global infrastructure, the massive computational power required to train and run complex models comes with a significant physical footprint.

Our project investigates how the AI boom drives the consumption of global electricity, water resources, and examines where this physical infrastructure is geographically concentrated.

Intended audience

Informed general readers, such as students and people interested in technology and sustainability, with no energy-sector background. The site explains terms like TWh, water stress and carbon intensity in plain language.

Questions

  1. How is the expansion of AI and data centres affecting global electricity and water consumption and carbon emissions, and which regions bear the heaviest environmental burden?
  2. How much electricity do data centres use today, and how fast is it growing?
  3. Which regions account for most of electricity, water?
  4. Where is the burden heaviest relative to local capacity? (based on electricity, water and CO2)
  5. How much can we attribute to AI, and how uncertain are the estimates?
